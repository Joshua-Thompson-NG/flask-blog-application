About Flask Blog

A simple blogging platform built with Flask, where users can register an account, write posts, and share what's on their mind.

What you can do here

  • ✓ Create an account and log in securely
  • ✓ Write, edit, and delete your own blog posts
  • ✓ Browse posts from other users on the home page
  • ✓ Update your profile picture and account details
  • ✓ Reset your password by email if you forget it

Built with

Flask Flask-SQLAlchemy Flask-Login Flask-WTF Flask-Bcrypt Flask-Mail Tailwind CSS

This project started as a way to learn Flask fundamentals: application factories, blueprints, database models, authentication, and form handling. Have a question or feedback? Get in touch.
